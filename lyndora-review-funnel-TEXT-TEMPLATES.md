## 📱 Text Message Templates

Copy and paste these templates when texting customers. Personalize with their name and vehicle details.

**Replace `[LINK]` with your actual short link (e.g., `bit.ly/lyndora-review` or `tinyurl.com/lyndora-review`)**

---

### Template 1: Short & Simple (Recommended)

```
Hi [Customer Name]! Congrats again on your [vehicle]! 🚗 
We'd love to hear about your experience. 
Quick feedback: [LINK]
```

**Example:**
```
Hi Sarah! Congrats again on your 2022 Honda Accord! 🚗 
We'd love to hear about your experience. 
Quick feedback: bit.ly/lyndora-review
```

## ⏰ When to Send

**Best Time to Send Initial Request:**
- **Day 1-2 after purchase:** Excitement is still high, car is new
- **Time of day:** 10 AM - 7 PM (avoid early morning or late night)
- **Best days:** Tuesday - Saturday (people are more relaxed)

**Follow-Up (if needed):**
- Wait **3 days** after initial message
- Send **only one** follow-up
- If still no response, let it go

---

## 📋 Salesperson Checklist

Before sending the text:

- [ ] Customer has left the lot with their vehicle
- [ ] You have their correct phone number
- [ ] You've personalized the message (name + vehicle)
- [ ] The link is correct (test it first!)
- [ ] You're sending during reasonable hours (10 AM - 7 PM)

---

## 💬 How to Respond to Their Replies

**If they reply positively:**
```
Thank you so much! Really appreciate it! 🙏
```

**If they mention an issue:**
```
I'm sorry to hear that. Let me get our manager involved 
to help resolve this ASAP. Someone will reach out shortly.
```
*(Then immediately notify the manager and forward the feedback email)*

**If they say "I'll do it later":**
```
No problem at all! Whenever you get a chance. 😊
```
*(Don't push - one reminder max)*

---

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

**Don't:**
- ❌ Send late at night or early morning
- ❌ Send multiple reminders (one follow-up max)
- ❌ Offer incentives ("Leave a review and get $20 off!")
- ❌ Pressure them ("We really need reviews!")
- ❌ Send generic copy-paste messages without personalization
- ❌ Text them weeks after the sale (strike while the iron is hot!)

**Do:**
- ✅ Keep it friendly and casual
- ✅ Personalize with their name and vehicle
- ✅ Send within 1-2 days of purchase
- ✅ Thank them for their business
- ✅ Make it easy (just click the link)
- ✅ Respect their time

## 🎯 Goal

**Target:** Get 1-2 Google reviews per week

**This means:**
- Send review requests to **every customer**
- Follow up once if no response
- Respond quickly to feedback
- Build your reputation over time

Consistency is key! Even if only 20-30% respond, that adds up to great reviews over time.
