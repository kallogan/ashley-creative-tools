# The Dream Life Goal

**Created:** 2026-05-06  
**Source:** Skill file from Kathy's training materials

---

## Overview

This is a motivational coaching wizard that helps you define your dream lifestyle down to the penny, calculates exactly how much you need to earn, and breaks it down into yearly, monthly, weekly, daily, and hourly income goals.

It then sets up daily and weekly reminders to keep you locked in and focused on your target.

---

## How It Works

### The Process

**Step 1: Dream Lifestyle Questions**

You'll be asked about:
- 🏠 Dream Home (monthly mortgage/rent)
- 🚗 Dream Vehicle (monthly payment)
- ✈️ Vacations & Travel (monthly budget)
- 🍽️ Food & Dining (monthly budget)
- 💪 Personal Trainer (monthly cost)
- 👨‍🍳 Personal Chef (monthly cost)
- 🎓 Kids' College (monthly savings)
- 📈 Retirement & Investments (monthly contributions)
- ➕ Everything Else (other monthly expenses)

**Important:** Give exact numbers down to the penny. No vague estimates. This is your DREAM life — think big!

**Step 2: The Big Reveal**

After answering all questions, you'll see:
- Your total monthly dream life cost
- Broken down into: yearly, monthly, weekly, daily, and hourly income targets

**Step 3: Set Up Reminders**

Two types of reminders:
1. **Daily Morning Reminder** — Every morning, reminder of your daily target
2. **Weekly Monday Check-In** — Every Monday, accountability check-in

You choose the times.

## Purpose

Most people work their whole lives without ever asking: *"How much does the life I actually want actually cost?"*

This exercise gives you:
✔️ A precise financial target based on YOUR dream life (not someone else's)  
✔️ Daily and weekly goals so you know EXACTLY what you need to earn  
✔️ Reminders that keep you motivated and accountable  
✔️ A coaching framework that makes your dreams feel achievable

---

## Notes

- This is a goal-setting exercise, NOT financial advice
- Be honest and specific — vague answers defeat the purpose
- If a category doesn't apply to you, it's okay to put $0.00
- You can always update your numbers later if your dream changes
- The reminders are designed to keep you energized and focused
